About Our Parish

One faith, one family, multiple communities united

Our Parish History

On July 1, 2024, Blessed Carlo Acutis Parish was born from the merger of two beloved faith communities: Our Lady of the Sacred Heart Parish in West Brookfield and St. Joseph Parish in North Brookfield.

This union represents not an ending, but a new beginning—a vibrant Catholic community that honors our rich heritage while embracing the future with hope and faith.

We celebrate Mass in two beautiful church buildings: Sacred Heart of Jesus Church in West Brookfield and St. Joseph Church in North Brookfield, each holding precious memories and continuing to serve as sacred spaces where we encounter Christ in Word and Sacrament.

Our Patron: Blessed Carlo Acutis

1991-2006

Carlo Acutis was a young Italian who loved the Eucharist and used his computer skills to create a website cataloging Eucharistic miracles around the world. He died of leukemia at age 15 and was beatified in 2020.

"To always be close to Jesus, that's my life plan." - Blessed Carlo

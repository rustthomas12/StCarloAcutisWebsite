Prayer Before Confession

O my God, I am heartily sorry for having offended Thee, and I detest all my sins because of thy just punishments, but most of all because they offend Thee, my God, who art all good and deserving of all my love. I firmly resolve with the help of Thy grace to sin no more and to avoid the near occasion of sin. Amen.

Holy Days of Obligation

Special celebrations in the liturgical year

  • Solemnity of Mary, Mother of God January 1
  • Ascension of the Lord 40 days after Easter (or 7th Sunday of Easter)
  • Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary August 15
  • All Saints Day November 1
  • Immaculate Conception December 8
  • Nativity of the Lord (Christmas) December 25

Mass times for holy days will be announced in the bulletin. If a holy day falls on a Saturday or Monday, the obligation to attend Mass may be abrogated.

Can't Attend in Person?

If you are unable to attend Mass in person due to illness or other circumstances, please contact the parish office about pastoral care and spiritual support.

Note: Watching Mass on TV or online does not fulfill your Sunday obligation unless you are prevented from attending due to serious reason.

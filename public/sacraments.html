The Seven Sacraments

Encountering Christ through the sacramental life of the Church

Baptism

Baptism is the first sacrament of initiation, washing away original sin and making us children of God and members of the Church.

Preparation: Parents and godparents must attend a baptism preparation class. Please contact the parish office to schedule: (508) 867-6469

First Holy Communion & Confirmation

Our Faith Formation program prepares children for these sacraments of initiation.

Registration: Contact the parish office for information about Faith Formation enrollment.

Reconciliation (Confession)

The sacrament of God's mercy and forgiveness.

Times: Saturdays 3:00-3:45 PM or by appointment

Full Schedule →

Holy Matrimony

Marriage is a sacred covenant between a man and woman, reflecting Christ's love for the Church.

Preparation: Couples must contact the parish at least 6 months before the desired wedding date. Pre-Cana preparation is required.

Contact: (508) 867-6469

Anointing of the Sick

This sacrament provides spiritual and sometimes physical healing for those who are seriously ill or preparing for surgery.

To Request: Please call the parish office: (508) 867-6469

Holy Orders & Religious Life

Feeling called to priesthood or religious life? We encourage you to pray and discern God's call.

Contact: Speak with Father Rainville or visit Worcester Diocese Vocations Office

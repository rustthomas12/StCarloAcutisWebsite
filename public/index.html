About Our Parish

Blessed Carlo Acutis Parish was formed on July 1, 2024, uniting the faithful communities of Our Lady of the Sacred Heart (West Brookfield), St. Joseph (North Brookfield), and St. John the Baptist (East Brookfield) into one vibrant parish family.

We celebrate the Catholic faith in two beautiful church buildings, embracing our rich heritage while looking forward to serving our community for generations to come.

Learn More About Us

Who is Blessed Carlo Acutis?

Carlo Acutis (1991-2006) was a young Italian who used his gifts in technology to spread devotion to the Eucharist. He was beatified in 2020 and is the patron of young people and the internet.
